FINANCIAL & ENGINEERING BENCHMARK

Why Modern Industry Chooses Aitmaad PEB Over Conventional RCC

A direct comparison between high-tensile steel engineering and outdated concrete columns.

Key Engineering Metric Conventional Concrete (RCC) Aitmaad PEB Steel Solution
Construction Speed Slow (8 - 14 Months). Wet curing, shuttering delays, weather sensitive. 40% - 60% Faster. Completed in 60 - 90 Days.
Unobstructed Clear-Span Limited to 10 - 15 meters. Internal columns block logistics forklifts and automation. Up to 60+ Meters Clear-Span with zero internal columns.
Seismic Performance Brittle and heavy. Higher risk of catastrophic shear fracture in earthquakes. High Ductility. Absorbs dynamic cyclic earthquake energy.
Dead Load & Foundation Immense mass (2400 kg/m³). Requires expensive deep pile foundations. 50% Lighter dead load = 30% saving on foundation civil works.
Relocation & Salvage Zero salvage value. Costly demolition and landfill waste. 100% Bolted Structure. Can be dismantled, expanded, or relocated.
