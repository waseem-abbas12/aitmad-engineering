Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

Custom-designed high-tensile steel building systems engineered for rapid delivery, cost efficiency, seismic resilience, and column-free spans up to 90 meters.

Engineering Superiority

Architectural Freedom with Industrial Strength

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) have revolutionized industrial construction across Pakistan. Unlike traditional hot-rolled sections or slow RCC casting, Aitmad PEB systems are engineered parametrically, where steel members are tapered according to the exact bending moment diagram.

This removes useless dead weight, reduces foundation loads by up to 35%, and guarantees 100% factory-controlled welding quality under certified American Welding Society (AWS) supervision.

Key System Capabilities:

Parametric tapered I-beam primary framing in ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel
Cold-formed galvanized Z and C purlins and girts for zero corrosion
Standing seam 360° mechanical lock roof sheeting with concealed clips
Clear spans up to 90 meters without intermediate columns
Integrated mezzanine floors and overhead crane runway beams up to 50 tons
Fast-track 60-90 day factory fabrication turnaround

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

PEB systems use high-tensile steel members tapered according to bending moment diagrams, reducing dead weight by up to 30%. They fabricate in controlled factory environments concurrently with foundation work, cutting total project completion time by 40-50% while offering superior earthquake resistance.

We regularly engineer and fabricate clear spans up to 75-90 meters without interior support columns. This provides 100% usable floor area for warehousing, logistics sorting, and aircraft hangars.

We utilize double-locked 360-degree standing seam metal roofing systems with concealed clips. Because fasteners do not penetrate the external roof sheet, thermal expansion does not cause elongation or water leakage.

Yes. Our engineers design the primary columns with stepped brackets or independent crane runway columns to support electric overhead traveling (EOT) cranes up to 50-ton capacity, fully compliant with CMAA guidelines.

Engineered to ASTM and AISC standards, our structures have a design service life exceeding 50 years. Periodic gutter cleaning and 10-year surface touch-ups are all that is typically required.
