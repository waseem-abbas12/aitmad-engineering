Engineering Heritage

Built on Trust. Engineered in Steel.

For over a quarter of a century, Aitmad Engineering has designed, fabricated, and erected heavy steel structures that form the backbone of Pakistani industry.

The Meaning Behind Our Name

"Aitmad" Means Unwavering Trust

In the steel and pre-engineered building industry, where projects are valued in hundreds of millions of rupees, trust is not an advertising slogan—it is a mathematical and operational imperative.

When a factory roof spans 70 meters over sensitive manufacturing equipment, or a blast-freezer cold room operates at -30°C in the blistering heat of southern Punjab, there is zero tolerance for deflected purlins, leaking fasteners, or compromised weld beads.

Aitmad Engineering was founded in 1998 with a clear conviction: to bring international standards of structural calculation, certified steel procurement, and robotic fabrication precision to Pakistan's burgeoning industrial landscape.

Our Mission

To engineer and deliver the safest, most value-optimized, and resilient industrial steel infrastructure in Pakistan through automated fabrication, transparent pricing, and zero-compromise safety.

Our Vision

To be the definitive engineering partner for regional conglomerates and multinational investors constructing complex manufacturing, energy, and logistics assets across South Asia.

Core Values

Absolute integrity in steel grades; millimeter tolerance in fabrication; unconditional safety for every worker on site; and enduring accountability for every bolt we install.

Evolution Over Time

Quarter-Century Milestone Journey

1998 — Foundation

Established with a single fabrication workshop in Lahore focusing on conventional roof trusses and small-scale textile sheds.

2005 — PEB Technology Adoption

Invested in automated submerged arc welding lines and imported high-precision cold roll-forming machines for Z/C purlins.

2012 — Sundar Industrial Estate Mega Plant

Relocated to a dedicated 120,000 sq. ft. modern facility in Sundar Industrial Estate, boosting monthly capacity to 1,200 metric tons.

2018 — Tekla LOD 400 & BIM Integration

Transitioned all structural detailing to 3D BIM, ensuring zero-clash pre-construction validation and flawless site bolt-ups.

2024 & Beyond — National Turnkey Leadership

Over 500 projects completed, 12.4 million square feet enclosed, and leading the transition to insulated standing seam envelopes.
