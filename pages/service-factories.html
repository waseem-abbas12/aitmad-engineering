Manufacturing Plants

Factories & Industrial Sheds

Production plants engineered around your assembly line layout with dynamic vibration control, heavy crane runways, ridge ventilation, and expandable end-walls.

Manufacturing Productivity

Engineered for High-Output Production Lines

Manufacturing facilities are complex machines. Every steel column placement impacts line efficiency, raw material movement, and operator safety.

Aitmad designs industrial sheds around client equipment drawings. Whether your facility houses high-speed weaving looms creating dynamic vibrations or heavy stamping presses requiring isolated slab joints, we integrate crane brackets, process piping supports, and natural daylighting seamlessly.

Key Factory Shed Capabilities:

Custom machine foundation isolation and trenching coordination
Dynamic load modeling for punching presses, looms and compressors
Natural gravity ridge ventilators and UV-stabilized polycarbonate daylighting strips
Overhead crane runways from 5-ton monorails to 50-ton double-girder EOTs
Substation, compressor room, and administrative mezzanine annexes
Expandable end-wall connections for easy future longitudinal growth
