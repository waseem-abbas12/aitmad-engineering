Technical Decision Matrix

Aitmad PEB vs. Conventional RCC Construction

Factor Aitmad Pre-Engineered Steel (PEB) Conventional Concrete (RCC)
Construction Timeline 3 to 6 Months (Simultaneous foundation & factory fabrication) 12 to 18 Months (Sequential curing and shuttering cycles)
Clear Span Usability Up to 90 Meters column-free (100% usable floor area) Limited to 15-20 meters before massive beams are required
Seismic Performance Superior: Low dead-weight and ductile moment connections High seismic risk due to heavy dead load mass
Foundation Cost 30% - 40% lighter foundation requirements Heavy, deep concrete piles and footing slabs required
Future Expansion Effortless modular expansion by adding end-wall bays Extremely difficult, costly, and structurally disruptive
