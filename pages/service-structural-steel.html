Heavy Industrial Fabrication

Structural Steel Fabrication & Erection

Heavy plate girders, pipe racks, heavy trusses, and multi-tier platforms built to AISC and AWS D1.1 standards for complex industrial processing facilities.

Precision Engineering

Certified Fabrication for Critical Plant Assets

From chemical and cement plants to fertilizer complexes and power generation substations, heavy industrial infrastructure requires uncompromising steel thickness, high-strength friction grip (HSFG) connections, and non-destructive weld testing (NDT).

Aitmad's Sundar facility features automated multi-torch CNC cutting tables capable of profiling plate up to 80 mm thick, paired with an enclosed blast booth ensuring Sa 2.5 profile depth for long-lasting high-build epoxy coating systems.

Key Fabrication Capabilities:

Heavy built-up plate girders, box columns, and lattice bridge trusses
Process industrial pipe racks, conveyor galleries and siloways
Multi-story composite commercial and institutional steel framing
Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI) and Radiographic weld testing
Automated shot-blasting to Sa 2.5 and specialized marine/chemical epoxy systems
Sequenced crane lift plans executed by certified riggers
