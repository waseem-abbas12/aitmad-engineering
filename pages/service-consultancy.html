Structural Value Engineering

Design, Engineering & BIM Consultancy

Parametric structural analysis in STAAD.Pro and Tekla LOD 400 detailing. We optimize steel tonnage by 12-22% through value engineering while maintaining full seismic safety margins.

Digital Precision

Eliminate Clashes Before the First Beam is Cut

Many structural consultants over-design steel frames with excessive uniform hot-rolled members because it is easier to calculate. This inflates project tonnage and wastes millions in raw steel costs.

Aitmad's design bureau performs rigorous finite-element modeling, tapering each member according to exact moment distributions. In Tekla Structures, we detail down to Level of Development (LOD) 400: every weld prep, stiffener plate, bolt hole, and anchor sleeve is modeled in 3D, guaranteeing 100% field fit-up with zero site torch cutting.

Consultancy Scope:

Parametric structural modeling using STAAD.Pro, ETABS, and SAP2000
Tekla Structures LOD 400 BIM modeling and automated CNC spool export
Seismic design compliance with Building Code of Pakistan (BCP 2021) and UBC 97
Value-engineering peer review audits reducing client steel tonnage by 12-22%
Anchor bolt setting templates and civil foundation interface coordination
Signed and stamped calculation dossiers by PEC Registered Professional Engineers
