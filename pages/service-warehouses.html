Warehouses & Cold Storage Solutions

High-bay logistics distribution centers and temperature-controlled cold storages engineered for maximum cubic storage efficiency, thermal envelope integrity, and automated material handling.

Logistics Engineering

Maximum Volumetric Capacity & Thermal Security

Modern logistics in Pakistan demands large, unobstructed clear spans capable of accommodating VNA (Very Narrow Aisle) high-density reach trucks and automated pallet shuttles.

In cold chain logistics (meat, dairy, citrus, mango exports, and temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals), Aitmad specializes in sub-zero thermal envelopes (-40°C blast freezers to +15°C staging areas). Our proprietary thermal break baseplate connections eliminate thermal bridging into the steel framework, preventing costly condensation and refrigeration energy loss.

Key Warehouse Capabilities:

High-bay racking integration with floor load capacities up to 10 ton/m²
Laser-screed FM2 flatness industrial floor slab coordination
Continuous PIR/PU sandwich panel wall and ceiling envelopes
Thermal break isolators preventing cold transfer to primary steel columns
Sealed docking bays with hydraulic levelers and industrial sectional doors
FM Global compliant fire suppression and smoke evacuation design
