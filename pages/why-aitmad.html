Corporate Credentials

The Bedrock of Industrial Trust

National certifications, international welding codes, audited safety records, and over 25 years of delivering high-stakes steel infrastructure across Pakistan.

Statutory Accreditations

Certified Engineering Authority

Our licenses and compliance systems ensure your facility meets both Pakistani regulatory requirements and international audit standards.

PEC Registration — Category C1

Registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) with specialized codes CE01 (Road & Bridges), CE10 (Structural Steelwork), and BC01 (Building Construction).

AWS D1.1 Certified Welding

All shop and field structural welding adheres to the American Welding Society Structural Welding Code. Every weld splice is visually checked and subject to ultrasonic testing.

ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 45001

Fully certified Quality Management System and Occupational Health & Safety management system guaranteeing traceable procurement and zero-accident job sites.

AISC 360 & MBMA Standards

All pre-engineered frames and steel calculations strictly follow American Institute of Steel Construction specifications and Metal Building Manufacturers Association guidelines.

Uncompromising Safety

Over 2,000,000 Safe Man-Hours

Industrial steel erection involves heavy tandem crane lifts, working at heights up to 24 meters, and high-wind weather challenges. At Aitmad, safety is an absolute core value, not a compliance footnote.

  • 100% 3rd-party certified mobile crane operators and rigging hardware
  • Full-body harnesses with 100% tie-off lifeline cables across all purlins
