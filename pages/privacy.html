Data Protection & NDA

Privacy & Information Security Policy

How Aitmad Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd. safeguards client project blueprints, commercial specifications, and corporate communications.

1. Professional Confidentiality (NDA)

Aitmad Engineering recognizes that industrial blueprints, proprietary production line layouts, site survey coordinates, and quotation tenders submitted to us contain sensitive commercial information. We treat all client submissions under standard professional Non-Disclosure principles. Your project drawings will never be published, licensed, or shared with third parties without written authorization.

2. Data Collection & Usage

When you request a quotation or contact our engineering bureau through this website, we collect your name, phone number, corporate email, group name, and building dimension specifications. This information is utilized strictly to formulate structural estimates, verify engineering parameters, and coordinate plant visits.

3. Cookie & Language Preference Policy

This website conducts no commercial tracking or advertising cookie syndication. We employ only local technical storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to retain your chosen interface language (English or Urdu) and preloader session state.

4. Inquiries & Legal Contact

For queries regarding data confidentiality or to execute a formal bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to sharing sensitive plant blueprints, please contact our legal team at inquiries@aitmadengineering.com.
