Building Envelope Engineering

Roofing, Cladding & Insulation

High-performance standing seam metal roofing, Zincalume single-skin profiles, continuous PIR sandwich panels, and energy-saving daylighting systems with zero fastener penetration leaks.

Weatherproofing Integrity

Zero Through-Fasteners. Total Thermal Shield.

Over 90% of industrial roof failures in Pakistan are caused by exposed screw fasteners. As high summer temperatures cause thermal expansion, screw holes elongate, washer gaskets dry rot, and water enters the building.

Aitmad's standing seam roofing solves this permanently. Panels are connected using concealed sliding clips and rolled together with an electric 360-degree mechanical seamer, creating a single continuous watertight steel membrane from ridge to gutter with up to a 20-year warranty.

Key Envelope Systems:

Aitmad-Lok 360° mechanical lock standing seam roof panels with concealed clips
Trapezoidal wall and roof profiles in 0.5mm, 0.6mm, 0.7mm Zincalume / Galvalume AZ150
Continuous-line manufactured PIR and PU sandwich panels with cam-lock options
High-density fiberglass wool insulation with reinforced white vinyl vapor barriers
UV-stabilized polycarbonate and FRP skylight sheets with matching rib profiles
Over-Roofing retrofits that install new standing seam roofs over old leaking sheds
