Transparent Methodology

The 6-Step Engineered Journey

How we take multi-million rupee industrial steel investments from initial concept drawings to certified, on-time turnkey facility commissioning.

01
Phase One

Technical Consultation & Feasibility

We initiate every project with a thorough engineering site assessment. Our team coordinates with your architects and production managers to understand floor clearance, crane runway requirements, wind/seismic zone classifications, and soil bearing capacity.

Typical Duration: 5 to 10 Working Days
Key Deliverables: Architectural GA Layout, Design Brief, Initial Budget Estimation
02
Phase Two

Parametric Structural Design & BIM Modeling

Using STAAD.Pro and ETABS, our licensed structural engineers calculate live, dead, wind, and seismic loadings per BCP 2021 and MBMA codes. Every beam, purlin, brace, and gusset plate is modeled in Tekla Structures to Level of Development (LOD) 400.

Typical Duration: 10 to 15 Working Days
Key Deliverables: Signed PE Calculation Dossier, Anchor Bolt Setting Plan, Tekla 3D Model
03
Phase Three

Automated Factory Fabrication

Fabrication takes place in our 120,000 sq. ft. Sundar Industrial Estate plant under strict ISO 9001 and AWS D1.1 quality supervision. Computerized plasma cutters slice high-tensile ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel plate, automated submerged arc welding lines fuse built-up members, and shot-blasting machines clean surfaces to Sa 2.5 profile.

Typical Duration: 30 to 45 Days
Key Deliverables: Mill Test Certificates (MTC), Ultrasonic NDT Weld Reports, Paint DFT Logs
04
Phase Four

Sequenced Logistics & Staging

Components are barcoded and bundled in strictly prioritized erection sequences (anchor bolts, main columns, rafters, purlins, and cladding). Dedicated articulated transport fleets deliver materials to project sites nationwide with zero site congestion.

Typical Duration: 7 to 14 Days
Key Deliverables: Packing Manifests, Safe Offloading Logistics Plan, Weatherproof Storage
05
Phase Five

Certified On-Site Erection & Cladding

Our certified erection superintendents mobilize heavy mobile cranes and telescopic boom lifts. Steel portal frames are raised, plumbed with laser transits, and secured with calibrated torque wrenches. Standing seam roofs are locked with automated 360-degree seaming machines.

Typical Duration: 30 to 60 Days
Key Deliverables: Safe Work Method Statements (SWMS), Daily Erection Logs, Calibrated Torque Checks
06
Phase Six

Quality Audit, Testing & Handover

Before commercial handover, we perform high-pressure water spray tests on roof flashings and verify crane runway alignment to millimetric tolerances. We deliver a complete closeout binder containing warranties, as-built BIM models, and preventative maintenance protocols.

Typical Duration: 5 to 7 Days
Key Deliverables: 10-Year Structural Integrity Warranty Certificate, As-Built Drawings, Closeout Dossier
