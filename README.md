# Aitmaad Engineering (اعتماد انجینئرنگ)
### Pakistan's Premier Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB) & Cold Storage Solutions

![Aitmaad Engineering](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1586528116311-ad8dd3c8310d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

A hyper-modern, ultra-responsive industrial web platform for **Aitmaad Engineering (Pvt.) Ltd.**, specializing in:
- **Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)**: Heavy industrial factory sheds, logistics warehouses, and clear spans.
- **Sandwich Panels**: Class B1 fire-rated continuous lamination Polyisocyanurate (PIR) and Polyurethane (PU) cores.
- **Controlled Atmosphere Cold Storages**: Sub-zero blast freezers (-40°C to +15°C) for mango/citrus export and pharma.
- **Turnkey Civil & MEP**: FM2 laser-screed jointless industrial flooring, substations, and NFPA fire hydrants.
